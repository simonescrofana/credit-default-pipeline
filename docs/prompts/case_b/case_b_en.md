# Case B — Prediction on Ad Hoc Data (English)

Ready-to-use prompts to test the agent on hypothetical companies, not present in the database. Copy and paste directly into the chat.

1. **High-risk scenario** (only the required fields)
   Assess the insolvency risk for a company founded on 2015-03-15, in the manufacturing sector, with 65% of the last 90 days' invoices unpaid and a total outstanding debt of €45,000.

2. **Low-risk scenario** (with more optional fields)
   Analyze a company founded on 2010-01-10, tech sector, Lombardia region, with only 5% of invoices unpaid over the last 90 days, a total outstanding debt of €2,000, an EBITDA of €80,000, a net profit margin of 12%, a cash-to-debt ratio of 1.5, and a leverage ratio of 0.8.

3. **Medium-risk scenario, with login activity**
   Consider a company founded on 2018-07-22, commerce sector, with 30% of invoices unpaid over the last 90 days, a total outstanding debt of €12,000, a leverage ratio of 2.1, 8 days since the last platform login, and a login velocity of 2 on a 0-3 scale.

4. **Scenario with active contracts and disputes**
   Evaluate a company founded on 2012-11-05, construction sector, Veneto region, with 40% of invoices unpaid over the last 90 days, a total outstanding debt of €18,500, 2 active contracts (gas and electricity), 3 billing disputes, and an average customer satisfaction score of 3.2 out of 5.
